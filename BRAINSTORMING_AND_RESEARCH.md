## 1. Executive Research & Competitive App Benchmarking

To build a world-class conference creation and audience growth platform for churches and ministries, we benchmarked against top event and creator platforms (**Luma**, **Eventbrite**, **Bevy**, **Splash**, **Hopin/RingCentral Events**, **Typefully**, **Blotato**, and **Webflow/Framer Design Libraries**).

| Feature Dimension | Luma (lu.ma) | Eventbrite / Bevy | Framer/Webflow Kits (Relume, Cruip) | Bent Planet (Our SaaS Moat) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual Aesthetics & Polish** | Minimalist glassmorphism, elegant typography, pastel/dark contrasts | Utilitarian, corporate, ticket-focused, dated layout | High aesthetic polish, bento grids, kinetic typography | **Church-Tailored High Aesthetic**: 6+ spiritual themes (Fire Revival, Cathedral Minimalist, Neon Youth, Global Summit), glass cards, ambient halos |

## 2. "10x Delighters" & Innovation Vectors for Church Conferences

### 1. Fixed But Flexible Wireframes (Customs)
- **The Core Problem**: Completely open visual website builders (like Wix or generic Webflow) overwhelm pastoral teams and result in broken layouts on mobile devices. Conversely, rigid forms (like Google Forms or basic Eventbrite) look generic, uninspired, and fail to evoke spiritual reverence.
- **The Bent Planet Solution**: A **Fixed But Flexible Wireframe Model**:
  - **Fixed (The Invariant Skeleton)**: High-converting faith layout architecture that attendees instinctively trust:
    1. Sticky Brand Navigation (Church Logo, Name, All Events portal)
    2. Hero & Spiritual Mandate (Atmospheric background, theme badge, bold title, host credit)
    3. Livestream & Countdown Dock (Working video player, countdown timer, replay vault)
    4. Spiritual Mandate & Context ("About This Gathering" with Scripture focus)
    5. Ministerial Roster (Featured Apostles, Pastors, Worship Leaders with bios and session tags)
    6. Time-Blocked Track Agenda (Multi-day or multi-session schedule with prayer/breakout badges)
    7. Free Study Material & Notes Vault (Lead magnet that captures subscribers)
    8. Sticky RSVP / Capacity Progress Bar + WhatsApp Direct Coordinator Chat
    9. Official Bent Planet Footer & Church Details
  - **Flexible (Customs)**:
    - 6 selectable wireframe topologies (Bento Grid, Cinematic Fire Revival, Editorial Cathedral, High-Energy Neon Surge, Executive Global Summit, Grace & Fellowship).
    - Block toggling: Toggle off stream player for in-person only summits, toggle off free resource if not provided, activate Prayer Request Wall.
    - Block reordering: Swap speaker spotlight above stream for minister-driven events.

### 2. AI Fine-Tuning directly into Wireframes
- Instead of generating raw markdown or a monolithic block of text, the AI engine directly **fine-tunes into the structured wireframe slots**:
  - `headline_hook`: High-impact spiritual proclamation tailored to the theme.
  - `scriptural_anchor`: Relevant Biblical reference that grounds the event.
  - `minister_bios`: Tailored biographies highlighting the speaker's calling and conference mandate.
  - `track_agenda`: Realistic time-blocked sessions (Consecration, Keynote, Altar Call, Impartation).
  - `whatsapp_broadcast_copy`: Ready-to-send broadcast message formatted with bold emojis.
  - `free_guide_concept`: Suggested study guide title to attract subscribers.

### 3. WhatsApp Community & Coordinator Sync
- 90%+ of church attendees in target markets (Africa, Latin America, UK, USA diaspora) coordinate via WhatsApp.
- Two distinct touchpoints:
  1. **Community Group Link**: One-click button to join the official conference WhatsApp group or channel for real-time announcements.
  2. **Click-to-Chat Coordinator**: Direct WhatsApp chat with the event host, pre-filled with:  
     `"Hi [Host], I want to register for [Conference Name] starting on [Date]."`

### 4. Interactive Live Prayer & Intercession Wall
- Attendees can submit prayer requests directly on the conference page.
- Pastoral teams can view prayer requests in their dashboard and mark them as "Prayed For 🙏", displaying an encouraging community counter on the public page.

### 5. Capacity Urgency & Seat Reservation Meter
- Luma-style limited capacity counter (e.g. `284 / 500 RSVPs Confirmed — 56% Filled`).
- Instills healthy urgency for high-demand revivals, youth retreats, and leadership retreats.
